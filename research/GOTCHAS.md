# Gotchas: traps that already cost us

- **A config 32/32 ranked on its tuning weeks can fail its first fresh week.** Weeks near 90% on time move ±3 points between settings by luck. Judge ranked share on fresh seeds only. (kit-live, vtest-1)
- **A gain measured on the weeks it was picked on shrinks on new weeks** (note reader +361 to +94, settings bundle +980 to +597). Prove on `h-` seeds.
- **A better forecast is not more margin.** Lane tables improved the win forecast and earned nothing. Always test live.
- **Models fitted under one fee policy mislead under another.** The booking model learned drivers who expect our raises; a flat-fee plan from it (`flatDays`) lost 1,570.
- **The same seed gives the same requests to every variant**, so base and variant weeks share their requests: do not count both when fitting on requests.
- **Requests of a lane table must come from seeds other than the test seeds** (typ.json: all bench seeds except `m-`, `h-`).
- **Report every week counted AND unranked = 0 (z).** One unranked week swings z by 300 over 48 weeks.
- **Do not refit the booking model on weeks played with our own policy.** Our schedules choose the fee from the very hazard being fitted (endogenous), so the refit predicts well on such weeks and plays 1,100 a week worse (model_dj, 14 Nov). Fit on random-fee exploration weeks.
- **One 32-week test at 2 SE is not proof.** The same knob step measured -54 ± 111 on t- seeds and +273 ± 112 on s- seeds (tuners o3, rs1, 14 Nov). Prove every accepted change on h- seeds.
- **Best-of-N picks are inflated (winner's curse).** Random search took the best of 4 three-knob moves (+432 ± 163, and a +364 ± 111 runner-up); both were -196 and +56 on fresh weeks. Prove the winner on new seeds, always.
- **LLM answers are slow in the tail and not repeatable.** Featherless: mean 0.9 s but a 5 to 19 s call in every week (the final gives 4.5 s); JEV repeats its pick 19 of 20 times. Keep answers in a file per seed (kit-llm `cache/jev/`) or the same seed is no longer the same week. JEV's confidence is low (mean 0.34): a gate at 0.8 switches it off.
- **A within-car comparison across variants is not a fee experiment.** Same seed + request under a higher opening fee shows more late refunds: the planner opens higher when the state is bad (confounded). Use knob-level differences, not realised fees. (research/views/open_fe.py, 14 Nov)
- **A learning knob with the wrong prior size reads as "no edge".** Measure the size of what is learned (SD in the right unit) before testing the learner. (dayLearn vs kit-price, 14 Nov)

- **Local sim accepts fractional minutes; the server does not (14 Nov).** Knobs in hours (rampWindowH, pairHoldH, minSlackH ...) must be whole minutes (x 60 an integer), or every week crashes at the first schedule ("is not a whole number", spread.ts checkWhole). kit-sim1 search values 9.771 / 5.576 crashed 48 server weeks; rounded to 9.75 / 5.6 / 2.95. Round search outputs before a server run.
- **The local sim (sim/, v4) overrates volume (14 Nov).** kit-sim1 (+25% orders) was +1,851 locally and +27 on the server with 13/48 weeks under 90% on time. Local searches must cap orders near the base, or count extra orders as a large cost; trust local gains only where orders stay flat (fee core: local +451, server +530).
- **Do not read a sweep table before it is full (14 Nov).** At 4 to 6 of 12 weeks three wild variants showed +320 to +420 (one at ± 44, 6/0); at 12 weeks they were +110 to +310. Early finishers are not a random sample of the seeds.
- **Fitting a sim to one more condition can break another (14 Nov).** v5c fitted the load response (held out: -293 vs server +183, v4 +1,973) and lost the fee and same-day response (ranking gate 15/17, corr 0.81 vs 16/17, 0.95). Rerun the ranking gate (`research/sim_calib/rankgate.py`) after every recalibration, and keep the old sim as a second judge.
- **A fit can match the totals with the wrong mechanism.** The first two-load fit (v5a) matched margin and on time by giving every driver 1 gig a day: 316 drivers a week vs 204 on the server, and half the gain vanished on held-out weeks. Put a structure target (drivers used) in the loss.
- **An unranked week = 0 score makes a sim search blind** (SE 500 on 96 weeks when 7% of weeks sit near 90%). Search on a smooth on-time penalty, judge the winner on the real rule.
- **The local sim overrates fee cuts after a drop (14 Nov).** A fresh, lower fee plan at a drop (`replanDrop`) was +257 ± 31 locally and +74 ± 94 / +14 ± 115 on the server. The sim's drivers take the dropped gig again at the lower fee; the server's do not. Sim gains on fee timing after drops need a server screen before any merge.
- **The local sim (v4) also overrates fee cuts and fee re-shaping, not only volume (14 Nov).** Fee tilt by gig size: sim +596 ± 50 with on time +1.0 point and flat orders; server 36 fresh weeks +47 ± 95 with on time -0.3 and one week unranked. "Orders flat" does not make a sim gain safe: prove every fee-side sim winner on the server.
- Sim (both worlds) says steeper fees after a deadline warning gain (steepOpen 1.76: +182 ± 63 held out, joint sets +385 to +639); the server says -126 ± 68 and -333 to +79 (fg-1..48, 14 Nov). Do not trust either sim world on the urgent-fee knobs (steepOpen, steepStep) or on joint sets built from them.
- **The local sim has no backup seller in an outage (14 Nov).** Its outage is a fitted day x place offset on the old win model, so a price above the competitor's level loses there. Outage pricing: old flag + backup price sim -126 ± 52, server +41 ± 92; region belief sim +154 ± 51, server +430 ± 144. Judge outage ideas on the server.
- **`week.ts` writes kit "kit-best" into bench.jsonl from every kit copy.** Rows of kit-out carry tags lo1, lp1 with kit "kit-best": tell them apart by tag.
- **Half-full again (14 Nov night):** fee_far at 16 of 32 weeks showed +223 / +469; at 32 weeks +73 ± 119 / +280 ± 131 with a ranked week lost. Never quote a half-full sweep as a size.
- **`pkill -f PATTERN` in a one-line shell command kills that shell too** (its own command line holds the pattern). Put kills in a script file.
- **Look at kit-best right before copying into it (14 Nov).** Another session made kit-best BEST13 while a test of mine ran for two hours on a copy of BEST12; my copy overwrote it for 5 minutes and replaced a backup file. Before a ship: read the ledger header again, `cmp` kit-best with the file the test kit was copied from, and never reuse a backup file name.
- **The local sim cannot judge anything the price filter learns about the competitor beyond week and weekday** (lane level: sim -123 ± 47, server +303 ± 83). Its competitor has no such structure.
- **Two sessions, one new folder name (15 Nov).** `rm -rf kit-safe && cp -r kit-best kit-safe` deleted the kit another session had made in the same minute; 24 of its practice weeks died. Check `ls -d` and the ledger's newest `open` rows right before creating a kit folder, and never `rm -rf` a kit you did not create in this session.
- **`runs/` was moved twice during the night of 15 Nov** (to `junction-2026/runs`, then `runs-2`). Kits recreate `Bahn-Spread/runs/` with an empty `bench.jsonl` and new slot locks: `cmp.py` then sees only the newest rows, and sessions no longer share locks. Compare with a tool that reads every `bench.jsonl`; do not move `runs/` while anything runs.
- **The runs folder moved twice on 15 Nov (00:57 and about 01:50 Helsinki)** while sweeps ran: rows of one test are split over `junction-2026/runs/bench.jsonl`, `junction-2026/runs-2/bench.jsonl` and `Bahn-Spread/runs/bench.jsonl`, and "done" log lines were lost with the folder. Compare across all three files.
- **A sweep run from `kit-best` changes model when `kit-best` is re-shipped (15 Nov).** Each week loads `algo.ts` when it gets its slot, so weeks still queued (or replays of 502-killed weeks) play the new defaults under the old label: BEST15 landed at 02:20 while `sf1 d11` / `d18` replays were waiting, 5 rows suspect. Run tests from a frozen copy (own kit folder), and check row times against the mtime of `algo.ts`. Server restarts (HTTP 502) also drop weeks silently: count rows per variant before reading a table.
